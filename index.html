My PM Philosophy: "Floor-First" Planning

   
       

I don't manage from a dashboard; I manage from the source.

       

When leadership sets aggressive timelines, my first step is to sit with the core technical teams - the engineers, testers, and fabricators - to map out the physical and technical constraints of the project.

       
       

By translating "floor-level" realities into data-driven, realistic timeframes, I protect the project from systemic failure and ensure that every deadline slapped on a sheet is backed by a technical consensus. I transform executive "orders" into executable "strategies."
